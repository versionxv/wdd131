Pokémon Reloaded

Pokémon Reloaded is a fangame of Pokémon made by Fer. The main gimmick of this fangame is the fact that battles are in real time. It also contains a good bunch of unique pokémon and a brand new region called Star.

My latest team. I'm palnning on having a whole team of double typing pokémon. Right now the team is a Fire/Fairy, Grass/Flying (will evolve and replace flying by ghost) and a Poison/Bug type. Eventually I'll add either a Fight/Psychic plus either of a Rock/Steel or Rock/Dark and depending of that choice a Steel/Dark. As you can notice I won't repeat typings, is a hard rule of mine in most Pokémon games.

The Map is HUGE!
Is extremely varied, lets you explore a lot and counts with all kinds of puzzles.
A wonder.

You can megaevolve pokémon!
Every megaevolution created until date and unique ones for the Star-Region Pokémon.

You can catch all Pokémons (1000+)!

This includes all legendary, all pseudo-legendary, all starters and a good bunch of unique and alternative forms.

A common thing is never running out of first time encounters for a very long time.

And there's a big history + several Pokémon League!

Right now the game currently has 5 Leagues (Star, Kanto, Johto, Hoenn and Sinnoh).

This of course includes all gym leaders of those regions and more are coming!
Being honest I have never beated all cause gets hard.
