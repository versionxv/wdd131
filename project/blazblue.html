BlazBlue: Central Fiction

Blazblue is my most played figthing game of all time. Is the game that finally gave me the chance of reaching high competitive level and the one that help me learn all the fundamentals of this genre.

The game is simple yet deep, with 36 total characters (AND a new one coming soon!!!). All have cool unique mechanic called "Drive" that is unique to each of them and make it so even characters you would believe to be played similar posses total different feeling and skill expression.

My main character is called Mai Natsume, a quick rushdown strike/throw character. Funny enough she breaks the system itself by having all her normals be follow up to each others whiff cancels, so yeah, that could be consider her unique driveness.
